A tiny friend at the top of your screen

Coucou watches your Claude Code, OpenCode and Antigravity sessions, lets you approve their requests from the island, and keeps an eye on Stripe, GitHub, Vercel, n8n and more. Free and open source.

Download for WindowsView on GitHub
Private by design. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your keys stay in Windows Credential Manager. Read the privacy policy.

Requires Windows 10/11. The installer isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a warning — click More info → Run anyway.
